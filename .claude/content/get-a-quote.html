What to include

The more of this you send in the first message, the closer the first number will be to the final one.

  • Artwork — JPG, GIF, PNG, BMP, PDF, AI, SVG, TIFF or PSD
  • Quantity — and the size breakdown if you have it
  • Garment — the blank you want, or tell us to suggest one
  • Placement and size — front, back, sleeve, and how big
  • Deadline — the date you need it in hand, not the date you would like it

What happens next

We come back with pricing and a timeline. Once you are happy and the invoice is settled, we send a digital proof with the final artwork. Nothing goes to press until you approve that proof.

Screen print production runs 10–20 business days from a paid invoice with all blanks on hand. Stickers and DTF print in 4–6 business days from proof approval, and anything ready before 12:00 PM CT dispatches the same day.

Over 2,000 units

Order totals are shown during ordering for everything up to 2,000 units, with nothing added beyond tax and shipping where applicable. Above 2,000 units — or for wholesale pricing — contact the sales team directly.
