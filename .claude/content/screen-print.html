Minimums, and why they exist

Screen printing means burning a screen per colour. That setup is fixed whether you print 24 shirts or 2,400, which is why there is a floor on quantity — and why the price per piece drops sharply as the run grows.

  • 24 pieces per design is the standard minimum
  • 72 pieces once artwork runs six colours or more
  • 36 pieces for jumbo placement prints
  • 72 pieces if you need fold and bag service

Need fewer than 24? That is what DTF transfers are for — no minimum, full colour, and no setup cost per colour.

Getting artwork right

No file gets turned away for being the wrong format, and there are no art fees. We accept JPG, GIF, PNG, BMP, PDF, AI, SVG, TIFF and PSD.

Three things make the difference between a good print and a great one: supply high-resolution images, avoid low-contrast designs that lose definition on fabric, and size the artwork in your design software exactly as you want it printed. Our team reviews every submission for print readiness before anything is burned.
