The process

DTF printing is a process where designs are printed onto a special film and then transferred onto fabric using heat. It allows for vibrant, full-colour prints with fine details and is suitable for a wide range of fabrics.

You will need a heat press or a household iron. For 100% cotton, we recommend pressing at 320°F for 12–15 seconds. For polyester or blends, use a lower temperature of 275°F for 10–12 seconds. Ensure you apply consistent pressure. Detailed application instructions are provided with each order.

Use high-resolution images and avoid low-contrast designs. If possible, size your design exactly as ordered in a design software. Our team also reviews each submission for print readiness.
