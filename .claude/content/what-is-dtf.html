What DTF actually is

DTF printing is a process where designs are printed onto a special film and then transferred onto fabric using heat. It allows for vibrant, full-colour prints with fine details and is suitable for a wide range of fabrics.

The practical difference from screen printing: there is no screen to burn per colour, so complexity costs nothing extra and short runs stay affordable. A single full-colour shirt costs what it costs.

How pricing works

Our pricing model is straightforward and transparent. We determine the cost based on the amount of space your design occupies on our sheets. This calculation is independent of the number of colours or the complexity of the design.

Which means you can arrange work to your advantage — tighter nesting on a gang sheet gives you more designs per sheet and a lower cost per design.

Pressing your transfers

You will need a heat press or a household iron. Detailed application instructions are provided with each order, but the settings that matter are:

  • 100% cotton — press at 320°F for 12–15 seconds
  • Polyester or blends — 275°F for 10–12 seconds
  • Pressure — apply it consistently across the whole design

Polyester needs the lower temperature because the fabric scorches and the dye migrates if you push it. If you are unsure what a garment is made of, test one transfer before committing the run.
