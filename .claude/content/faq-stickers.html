Changing or cancelling

Once your order is placed, changes can only be made if the printing process has not started. Contact customer service immediately with the details of your order if you need to make adjustments.

If your order has not already been printed, you can contact us by filling out our contact form or emailing info@rodinkprinting.com. If your email is received before production has started, we will issue a refund and cancel the order. However, we print all orders placed before noon the same day, so we recommend contacting us quickly.

Turnaround & shipping

Typical orders take 4–6 business days (Monday to Friday) to print, after proof approval.

Place your order before 12:00 PM CT and we dispatch it the same day. This applies to all orders, including UPS Overnight and UPS Second Day Air. Orders received after the cutoff ship the following business day.

We offer standard and expedited shipping options. Shipping times and costs vary based on your location and the size of your order. All shipping options are available at checkout.

You will automatically receive an email with tracking information upon shipment. If you believe there is an issue with your order shipment, please contact us and a customer service representative will assist.

If your order is delayed, please contact our customer service team for assistance. We will work with the shipping carrier to resolve any issues.

Problems with an order

We know that nothing is more frustrating than an order missing an item. Contact a member of our support team with your order number and we will resolve the issue.

If you have received an order and believe there has been an error with the printing or an issue with the quality, contact a member of our support team and we will resolve it.
