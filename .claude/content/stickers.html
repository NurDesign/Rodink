Why stickers work

Stickers are a fast and easy way to promote your business, brand or event. Thick, durable vinyl protects your stickers from scratches, water and sunlight.

Any shape, no surcharge

We cut your sticker exactly to your design for no additional charge. There is no premium for an awkward outline and no reason to force your artwork into a circle because it is cheaper. Design away.

How pricing works

Price is determined by the amount of space your design occupies, not by how many colours it uses or how complicated the shape is. Quantity is the other lever — the more you order, the lower the unit price. Totals are displayed for orders up to 2,000 units, with nothing added beyond tax and shipping where applicable.

Proofs and cutlines

You will receive a proof within 24 hours of placing your order, showing the final artwork with the cutline drawn on it. That cutline is the exact path the blade follows, so it is worth a careful look. Nothing prints until you approve it.
