About

A print shop in Los Angeles.

RodinK is a custom garment decorating and fulfillment company, centrally located in Los Angeles, California, running a fully automated facility built around screen printing.

Work with us See the work

What we do

We decorate garments and fulfil the orders that come off them. The facility is fully automated and runs current screen printing technology, which is what lets us hold a consistent hand across a 24-piece order and a several-thousand-piece one.

Screen printing is the core. Custom stickers and direct-to-film transfers came later, and they exist for the jobs screen printing is the wrong tool for — short runs, photographic artwork, and fabrics that do not take plastisol well.

Who we work with

The range is unusually wide for a shop our size, and that is deliberate. Working across very different customers is what has made us one of the more diverse screen printing companies in the industry.

  • Some of the largest streetwear brands
  • Local gyms, teams and fight clubs
  • Licensed products
  • Corporate identity and uniform programmes
  • Local retailers
  • Trade show and event giveaways

Understanding where styles are heading is part of the job. It is how we keep customers current with what is actually selling rather than what sold two seasons ago.

We design too

We work with a lot of different companies and businesses creating logos and illustrations. If you need a logo, a T-shirt graphic or lettering, get in touch — we would genuinely like to hear about it.

We are also always looking for new opportunities to work on creative projects with talented people. If you are interested in collaborating, contact us.

How we price

No art fees, on any service. Screen printing is priced on quantity, colour count and placement. Stickers and DTF are priced on the area your artwork occupies — not on how many colours it uses or how complicated the shape is. Order totals up to 2,000 units are shown during ordering, with nothing added beyond tax and shipping. Above that, we quote directly.
